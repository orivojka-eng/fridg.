Support

Email support@fridgr.app — we answer within two working days. In the app: Profile → Support sends a message with your app version attached.

Quick answers

The scan did not recognise something

Tap the item chip to deselect it, or add it by hand. Every scan is a suggestion until you confirm it.

I have an allergy — is it safe?

Allergies in your Food Profile are hard rules for generated recipes, but always check packaging yourself. Imported recipes show a warning when they contain one of your allergens.

How do I cancel Premium?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period.

Restore a purchase on a new phone

Open Premium in the app and tap "Restore". Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Delete my account

Profile → Delete account. Everything is removed immediately, including Sign in with Apple access. You can export your data first from the same page.

Reminders are not arriving

Check Preferences → Notifications in the app and that FRIDGR is allowed to notify in your phone settings.

Report a problem

Send the steps you took, what happened and what you expected to support@fridgr.app. Screenshots help.
