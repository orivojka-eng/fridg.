3. Legal bases (GDPR)

Contract (running the service you signed up for); consent (notifications, camera, diagnostics — withdraw any time); legitimate interest (security, abuse prevention, aggregate statistics). Dietary preferences are processed on the basis of your explicit consent given when you fill in the Food Profile; you can clear them at any time in Preferences.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Neon (database hosting, EU region)
  • Anthropic (AI recipe generation and food recognition; receives fridge items, dietary rules and scan photos — never your name or email)
  • Cloudflare (temporary photo storage, EU)
  • Resend (transactional email)
  • Expo (push notification delivery)
  • RevenueCat, Apple, Google (subscriptions)
  • Fly.io (API hosting, EU region)

Each receives only what its task requires under a data processing agreement. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

5. Your rights

Access, correct, export (Profile → Delete account → Export my data), delete (Profile → Delete account: immediate and permanent, including revoking Sign in with Apple), restrict or object, and complain to your supervisory authority. Email privacy@fridgr.app; we answer within 30 days.

6. Security

Encrypted in transit (TLS), passwords hashed with Argon2id, tokens stored in the device keychain, secrets never shipped inside the app, access logged and rate-limited.

7. Children

FRIDGR is not directed at children under 13 (16 where the law requires) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app before they take effect and keep the date at the top current.
